# DeFi Stress Lab
### Stress Testing Liquidity and Risk in Automated Market Makers


**Project Overview:**
Our goal is to bridge traditional quantitative risk management techniques and DeFi through an interactive tool. DeFi Stress Lab is designed to serve as a simulation platform to evaluate how decentralized exchanges react to extreme market scenarios. There will be options to simulate sudden price changes, liquidity shortage, and arbitrage activities on the platform. Users will be able to understand what kind of risks automated market makers and liquidity providers may face under different conditions.
